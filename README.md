## 📌 Executive Summary

This interactive **Financial Dashboard** built in Power BI provides a comprehensive operational and financial performance overview across major platforms and revenue channels, including **Walmart, Amazon, Costco, Uber, Freelance**, and salaried streams. 

The dashboard presents key metrics such as total income, total expenses, net balance, regional transaction distributions, and preferred payment methods in a clear visual layout.

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations & Breakdown

1. **Monthly Expense Dynamics (Waterfall Chart):**
   * Displays month-over-month expense fluctuations from January to December, highlighting percentage increases and decreases across the calendar year[cite: 6, 7].
2. **Income & Expense by Store (Clustered Column Chart):**
   * Compares total income against operational expenses across specific entities: *Salary, Freelance, Uber, Local Market, Amazon, Costco*, and *Walmart*[cite: 6, 7].
3. **Turnover by Payment Method (Donut Chart):**
   * Illustrates overall transaction volume grouped by payment type: *UPI, Cash, Card*, and *Bank Transfer*[cite: 6, 7].
4. **Regional Breakdown (Transaction Summary Table):**
   * Summarizes high-level bank transfer figures across geographical regions (*East, North, South, West*), totaling `$3,962,068.80`[cite: 6, 7].
